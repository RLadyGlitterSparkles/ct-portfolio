Reflection

I wanted to analyse the Spotify data set in order to determine the different popular songs and their streams. I made many scorecard charts exhibiting the most popular songs of different years based on streams. I also studied the effect of different beats per minute on the amount of streams the song gets. I concluded that on average, the higher the bpm was, the more streams it got, most likely because it created a faster pace making the song engaging. With further research I discovered that faster tempos raise excitement and mimic an active heart rate which makes the listener feel more alert. It's most commonly listened to when people are exercising, driving or cleaning as it keeps their motivation high. It is also extremely predominant in platforms such as Tiktok and Instagram when featuring rapid visuals. It also delivers hooks and drops in the song earlier, targeting the shorter attention spans of music listeners. I also examined the keys in which songs are played in order to try and determine if there was a most popular key, and while the results were that there was around an even spread of different keys, the main one was in the key C because it's relatively easier to play in. The even spread of keys could be based on the singer's range or instrument being played. For example, its is easiest for horns to play in the key F or B-flat. This research could have been conducted better if I had put different facts on the scorecard charts because currently they are interesting, but repetitive. I also could have created a chart that showed the effect of the key of a song on the streams it got. In conclusion, this research task demonstrates my ability to turn data into information, but could have been improved slightly.
